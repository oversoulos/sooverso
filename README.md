# So Over So

Not a marketplace. Not another shopping app. A head-to-head promotion platform for people who don't get seen.

Two products enter. One gets chosen. The loser disappears. The winner gets the spotlight — and the sale.

Built for indie merchants, side hustlers, creators, and anyone tired of begging algorithms for visibility.

---

## What This Is

A low-risk, high-visibility way to promote products. No affiliate marketing. No expensive storefronts. No pay-to-play nonsense.

You list two products. People pick their favorite. The winner gets pushed into a fast, clean checkout — and your storefront gets the traffic.

We don't keep your customers. We send them to you.

---

## Who This Is For

- **Merchants** who can't afford visibility but have good products
- **Shoppers** who want something more fun than scrolling through infinite grids
- **Community builders** who want to moderate, review, and help shape something new
- **Collaborators** who see the potential in competitive, reward-driven shopping

This is not a passive experience. You participate. You get rewarded. You help decide what wins.

## For Merchants (Free Tier)

- List up to 3 head-to-head duels per month
- Set your own products, quantities, and rotation schedule
- Choose how the loser exits (trash can, dramatic fall, your own animation)
- See win/loss ratios and conversion data
- Pay only a small service fee on sales — no upfront cost

**Subscription Tier adds:**
- Unlimited duels
- Full analytics dashboard
- Custom branding and themes
- Bonus wheel, bargain pop-ups, and priority rotation
- Storefront redirect after purchase
